# Sift v1.2.0

A fix for uninstalling apps that Sift wrongly thought were still open, and a new Rescan button so Sift can catch up with changes you make outside it.

## What's new

- **Uninstalling no longer asks you to quit an app that isn't open.** On a Mac, some apps leave a small helper running in the background, like an updater or something that starts when you log in. It has no window and no Dock icon, but Sift counted it as the app being open and told you to quit first, with nothing to quit. Sift also kept showing an app as "Open now" if it was open when you first visited Apps, even after you'd closed it. Now Sift checks again when you click Uninstall, and only the app itself counts as open. If one of its background helpers is still running, Sift stops it before uninstalling. As before, the app and everything it left behind go to Restore for 30 days.

- **Rescan whenever you like.** Sift looks around once when you open it. If you delete, move or download things afterwards, in Finder or another app, click **Rescan** under your drive in the sidebar, or choose **Rescan** in quick search (⌘K / Ctrl K). Home keeps showing your last results and the progress while Sift looks, then updates every page. A rescan only looks. It doesn't remove anything.

## One thing worth knowing

A full rescan walks your whole home folder again, so on a big drive it takes about as long as the first look did. Things you remove inside Sift already update right away, so you only need Rescan for changes made elsewhere.

## Works on

- **macOS** 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- **Windows** 10+ (x64 and ARM64)
- **Linux** (x86_64)
